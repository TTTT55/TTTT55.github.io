Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This document explains how Fluxify handles information when you use the application.

Application: Fluxify
Effective Date: November 11, 2026
Last Updated: November 11, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Fluxify, an Android application developed and maintained by its independent developer ("we", "our", or "us").

Fluxify provides Android interface customization and system UI personalization features. We aim to minimize the collection and processing of personal information and prioritize on-device functionality wherever possible.

2. Information Processed by the Application

Fluxify does not require users to create an account or submit personal information to our own servers.

Depending on the features enabled and permissions granted, the application may process the following information locally on the device:

  • Application preferences and customization settings.
  • Device and system information required for compatibility.
  • Information accessed through user-enabled Android system permissions and services.

This information is processed on the device to provide the application's features. The application does not maintain its own server-side database of users' personal information or notification contents.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Fluxify uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements to users of the application.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain information to deliver advertisements, measure advertising performance, improve services, and prevent fraudulent activity.

Depending on the SDK configuration and applicable settings, this information may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • Android Advertising ID and other device or account identifiers.
  • Advertising interactions, including ad impressions, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as app launch time, crash-related information and SDK performance.

This information may be processed by Google and relevant advertising service providers in accordance with their respective privacy policies and applicable settings.

Depending on the user's location, applicable consent requirements, device settings and advertising configuration, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

We do not directly operate Google's advertising infrastructure or independently control the information collected by its advertising SDK.

For more information, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information for Advertising

4. Accessibility and Notification Permissions

Certain Fluxify features may require Android Accessibility Service or Notification Listener access, depending on the features used by the user.

These permissions are used to provide requested customization and system interface functionality. Information accessed through these services is processed locally for those features and is not transmitted to our own servers.

Fluxify does not intentionally store notification text or personal notification contents.

Users can revoke these permissions at any time through Android system settings. Some application features may stop functioning after permission revocation.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

We do not operate our own user-data sharing infrastructure. However, information collected through Google AdMob may be shared with Google and relevant advertising partners as necessary for advertising delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention.

6. Data Storage and Security

Application customization preferences are stored locally on the user's device.

We do not maintain a centralized database containing users' personal information, notification contents or personal files.

Data transmitted by Google's advertising SDK is handled according to Google's security practices, including encryption in transit where supported by the SDK.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Local application preferences remain on the device until they are cleared by the user or the application is uninstalled, subject to Android storage behavior.

We do not retain users' personal information on our own servers because we do not operate a user account or personal-data storage backend.

Information collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's applicable data retention and privacy policies.

8. Children's Privacy

Fluxify is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers.

Advertising services may apply their own age-related protections and restrictions where required by law.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Users can exercise control over their privacy by:

  • Revoking optional application permissions through Android settings.
  • Managing advertising privacy and Advertising ID settings through Android settings.
  • Managing applicable advertising consent choices where presented.
  • Clearing application data or uninstalling Fluxify to remove locally stored application preferences.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application functionality, advertising services or applicable legal requirements change.

Any updates will be published on this page along with a revised effective date.
